Data annotation and agreement. Pass mark: 70 %.

:::quiz
? Which three ingredients does an annotation project need?
+ a label scheme, a guideline, and annotators
- a GPU, a neural network and a test set
- a SUS questionnaire, a timer and a rubric
> Plus usually a small annotation tool.

? Annotation belongs to which step of the ML workflow?
+ Create training material
- Evaluate model
- Adjust and optimise
> Labels are the training material of supervised learning.

? Percent agreement is 0.70 and chance agreement is 0.40. Cohen's kappa?
= 0.5
> (0.70 − 0.40) / 0.60 = 0.5.

? Kappa close to 0 means…
+ agreement no better than chance
- perfect agreement
- the annotators never agree
> κ ≈ 0: the observed agreement is what label frequencies alone would produce.

? Annotators often confuse “Shirt” and “T-shirt/top”. Best first reaction?
+ Add examples and a clear rule for this pair to the guideline (or merge the labels)
- Fire the annotators
- Ignore it, the model will fix it
> Systematic disagreement points to an unclear guideline or scheme.

? Annotator A says YES for 50 % of items, annotator B for 50 %. Chance agreement p_e?
= 0.5
> 0.5·0.5 + 0.5·0.5 = 0.5.

? Why can a model not be expected to be “more correct” than the agreement between human annotators?
+ The labels themselves are uncertain; the model learns from (and is scored against) noisy human labels
- Because models are always worse than humans
> Agreement is a ceiling on meaningful evaluation.
:::
